FuXi Mini Pipeline Overview
===========================

1. Data Preparation (`sample_zarr_to_netcdf.py`)
-----------------------------------------------
- Reads ERA5-style Zarr samples (pressure-level and surface fields).
- Pressure fields: temperature, geopotential, u, v at 13 levels → 52 channels.
- Surface fields: 2 m temperature & humidity, 10 m wind (u/v), surface pressure → 5 channels.
- Currently captured channels: 57 (13 specific-humidity levels still missing for full 70).
- Packs tensors as `(time, channel, lat, lon)`, slices a mini grid `(16, 8)`, saves `mini_sample.nc`.

2. Cube Embedding (`fuxi_wp/cube_embedding.py`)
----------------------------------------------
- Expects `(B, C, T=2, H=16, W=8)` history cubes.
- Single 3D conv (kernel `(2,4,4)`, stride `(1,4,4)`) shrinks spatial resolution ×4 while keeping history.
- Kaiming init; produces `(B, 384, H'=4, W'=2)` latent “cube” features.

3. Swin Transformer Stack (`fuxi_wp/swin_blocks.py`)
----------------------------------------------------
- Wrapper around Microsoft Swin-v2 `BasicLayer`.
- Operates on `(B, H', W', C)` tokens with alternating shifted windows.
- Auto-clamps window size to fit mini grid; mini config uses depth=2, heads=4, window=2.

4. FuXi Tiny Backbone + Decoder (`fuxi_wp/fuxi.py`)
---------------------------------------------------
- Embeds history with `CubeEmbedding3D`.
- Applies two SwinBlockStack stages (encoder + bottleneck) at `(4, 2)`.
- `UpsampleDecoder` (conv + transpose-conv) upsamples to `(16, 8)` and predicts output channels.
- End-to-end forward returns `(B, C_out, 16, 8)`; C_out defaults to input channel count.

Training Loop (`fuxi_wp/training.py`)
-------------------------------------
- Loads `mini_sample.nc`, renames dims, stacks variables, normalizes per-channel (z-score).
- Dataset yields `(history, target)` tensors; DataLoader shuffles mini batches.
- `FuXiTinyBackbone` instantiated with dataset channel count and mini spatial grid.
- Runs 5-epoch toy loop with AdamW + MSE to confirm gradients.

Architecture Schematic (Mini Config)
------------------------------------
Input (B, 70, T=2, H=16, W=8)
 └─ CubeEmbedding3D            → (B, 384, 4, 2)
 └─ Rearrange                  → (B, 4, 2, 384)
 └─ SwinBlockStack #1          → (B, 4, 2, 384)
 └─ SwinBlockStack #2          → (B, 4, 2, 384)
 └─ Rearrange                  → (B, 384, 4, 2)
 └─ UpsampleDecoder            → (B, 70, 16, 8)
